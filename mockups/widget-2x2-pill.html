2×2 widget with the car pill — layout options

The pill (variant C) is easy on 4×2 and 4×3 because those headers already have a model line. 2×2 has no spare row, so something has to move. Each option is shown at a typical 168px cell and at the 118px minimum, to expose clipping.

Line 1: car pill + range. Line 2: big SOC. Buttons unchanged. Costs ~14dp of height, which the normal cell has.
