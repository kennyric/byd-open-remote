Same icon in the swipeable vehicle card, so the car is recognizable at a glance while switching.

Storage & scope. Emoji is a short string stored per VIN next to the custom name. Photos are decoded, center-cropped square, downscaled (~256px) and saved under app files keyed by VIN, so no gallery permission or URI is kept. Emoji also works in home-screen widgets (plain text); photos would only render in the app unless widgets get a bitmap path. Widget support for photos is a separate decision.
